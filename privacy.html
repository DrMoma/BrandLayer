Privacy notice.

This page explains what data brandlayer.no collects, how it's used, and your rights under GDPR. Brand Layer is the data controller; questions go to hello@brandlayer.no.

1. Who we are

MOMCILO PAUNOV, organised as an Enkeltpersonforetak (ENK) in Norway. Organisasjonsnummer 936 104 894. Registered address: Vinkelveien 5, 3027 Drammen, Norway. Trading as Brand Layer. Contact: hello@brandlayer.no.

2. What we collect

  • Booking data — when you book a call via our embedded Cal.com widget, Cal.com collects your name, email, the answers you give to our booking questions, and your time zone. We see this so we can prepare for the call.
  • Email — when you write to hello@brandlayer.no, we store your message and reply via standard email. Mail is forwarded through Cloudflare Email Routing to a Google (Gmail) inbox.
  • Server logs — Vercel (our host) logs requests including IP address, user agent, and referrer for ~24 hours for abuse-prevention purposes.
  • Fonts — DM Sans + DM Mono are self-hosted on our own domain; no font requests are sent to third parties.
  • No analytics, no advertising trackers, no marketing pixels.

3. Legal basis

  • Performance of a contract (when you book a call or hire us) — GDPR Art. 6(1)(b).
  • Legitimate interest (server logs for security, replying to email enquiries) — GDPR Art. 6(1)(f).

4. Who we share data with

  • Cal.com (US-based; processes EU/EEA bookings under Standard Contractual Clauses).
  • Vercel (US-based; hosts the site; processes under Standard Contractual Clauses).
  • Google (Gmail — receives and stores email sent to hello@brandlayer.no).
  • Cloudflare (DNS, email routing).
  • We do not sell or rent personal data to anyone.

5. Retention

  • Booking records: kept while the working relationship is active, then deleted within 12 months.
  • Email correspondence: kept up to 3 years after our last exchange, then deleted.
  • Server logs: rotated automatically by Vercel within ~24 hours.

6. Your rights under GDPR

You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or port your personal data, and to object to processing. To exercise any of these, email hello@brandlayer.no — we'll respond within 30 days.

If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet) or your local supervisory authority.

7. Cookies

Our site itself sets no cookies. The embedded Cal.com booking widget sets functional cookies once you interact with it — see Cal.com's privacy policy for details.

We may update this notice as our tools or practices change. The current version is dated below.
